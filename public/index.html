DETAILS
Before you connect.

    
What exactly does holding unlock?+
A maximum daily allowance of AI input and output tokens, shared across your API keys. The Solana coins stay in your wallet. Daily and shared-pool caps still apply, and service pauses when funding or capacity is unavailable. The final threshold will be published before launch.

    
What happens when I sell?+
Holdings are checked again before each new API request. Below the required amount, your key cannot make new calls. Calls already in progress may finish. Creating another key does not reset the wallet’s daily allowance.

    
Who processes prompts and responses?+
Slipvolt forwards requests through OpenBroker to the selected Gonka model provider. Review the privacy page before sending personal, confidential, or regulated data.

    
Where does the GNK come from?+
The operator funds the native-GNK treasury and allocates a finite inference budget. Collected creator fees may replenish it after disclosed operating costs. Swap execution and fee collection are not automated in this build.

    
How is the developer paid?+
Developer compensation would come from disclosed project revenue after service costs, customer obligations and reserve replenishment. Final compensation, retained-fund and optional burn percentages will be published before launch. No payouts are automated.
