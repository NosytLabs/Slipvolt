Prelaunch service rules

This is a draft of service behavior and launch disclosures, not a finalized sales contract. No token purchase or paid checkout is offered by this build. The operator must publish final terms, an identified service provider and a private contact before accepting customers or funds.

What holding gives you

A qualifying wallet can create revocable customer API keys and request a finite, funded AI allowance. It is not a treasury share, prepaid deposit, GNK ownership claim, dividend or redemption right. There is no guaranteed return, token price, resale liquidity or uninterrupted access.

Published wallet/global quotas, model limits, current holdings, account status, maintenance, capacity and GNK availability govern access. Unused daily allowance has no rollover or cash redemption. Holding more than the threshold does not automatically multiply the allowance.

No staking contract, token-payment settlement or automated treasury swap is implemented. A research document or planned feature is not a working customer benefit. A token’s identity requires a verified mint address; a ticker alone is not enough.

Use the service responsibly

Use wallets and content you are authorized to use. Protect API keys and do not bypass quotas, attack the service, rotate wallets to drain the shared fund, or pass private operator credentials to other users. Account controls let the operator suspend new requests when necessary.

Review model output before using it. Models can be wrong, omit important details or generate unsafe code. Your application must validate tool arguments and authorize actions; model output does not authorize a transaction or a payment.

Charges, failures and refunds

Holder access has no second payment step in this build. The displayed metered price is a proposed rate card; automatic checkout and overage billing are disabled. A market token purchase does not create service credit.

Uncertain provider results can leave allowance reserved pending review. Cancellation is not proof of zero upstream cost. Key revocation does not delete prior usage or stop work already dispatched.

No refund workflow or paid-service refund policy is implemented. Final payment terms must describe unused credits, verified billing errors, failed delivery, refunds and support before payment collection is enabled. This draft does not declare all sales final.

Compute funding and developer compensation

The operator allocates native GNK to serve inference. Funding may come from operator capital, collected creator fees or future paid usage. Buying tokens on a market is not a direct deposit into the service’s compute budget.

The proposed policy allocates 70% of distributable surplus to the developer and retains 30%, only after service costs, customer obligations and reserve replenishment. That is not 70% of all token purchases. It is not a holder profit share, and no payout is automated. Actual allocations and costs need operator records; market capitalization is not revenue.

What must be published before launch

The final mint and buy route, holder threshold and allowance, funding policy, team allocations and vesting, applicable fees, operator identity, private support, privacy/retention details, payment/refund terms and a policy-change notice process are still launch requirements.
