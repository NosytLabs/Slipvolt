A burn budget is cash you might spend buying tokens. No number of tokens burned or price increase is inferred; actual execution needs a quote, authorization and an on-chain burn receipt.

Count money that can pay the bill.

Eligible trading volume is neither market capitalization nor money raised by the project. Use the creator share after any fee sharing, and the fraction actually collected. The default 30 basis points means 0.30%, not Pump’s entire trading fee.

Operating costs should include hosting, RPC, support, swaps, gas, refunds and tax obligations. Enter provider charges not included in per-request usage separately. Fund your service reserve before optional burns or developer distributions.

Keep principal out of revenue.

User lock deposits, refundable balances, unclaimed fees, unspent customer prepayments, and unsold project tokens are not earned operating revenue here. A staking vault is separate from the developer-managed treasury.

Burning reduces token supply. Locking removes tokens from circulation temporarily. Neither creates GNK nor guarantees trading demand, profit or token appreciation.
